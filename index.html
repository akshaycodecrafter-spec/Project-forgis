// 01 / The Club

$ man forgis

FORGIS opened in 2015 as six racks in an Al Quoz warehouse. The premise hasn't changed since: every session programmed, coached, and logged — reviewed against hard numbers each quarter. We're not louder than other gyms. We're just harder to outgrow.

  • [+]Programming over vibes — every block written, logged, and reviewed.
  • [+]Coaching ratios capped at 1:8, enforced by the booking system.
  • [+]Quarterly benchmarks — strength, conditioning, body comp. On paper.
